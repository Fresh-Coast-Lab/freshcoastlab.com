The MSPs winning right now automate the boring stuff.

What a week at Kaseya Connect made obvious.

Wheels up from Vegas after Kaseya Connect, and one thing was clear from every hallway conversation: the MSPs pulling ahead aren't the ones with the biggest stacks.

They're the ones who automated the boring stuff so their techs can do real work.

Boring is where the time goes

Password resets. Onboarding checklists. Patch reports nobody reads. License reconciliations. None of it is hard. All of it eats hours. And every hour a senior tech spends on it is an hour they're not spending with a customer.

The best shops I talked to picked one of those jobs, automated it end to end, and then checked that it actually worked. Then they picked the next one.

The part people skip

Automation that nobody watches is just a new way to fail quietly. I learned that at home when a camera stopped reporting motion for six days and every dashboard still said healthy. So whatever you automate, give it a watchdog. Something that notices when the thing that should happen, didn't.

Automate the boring stuff. Watch the automation. Spend the time you get back with customers.
