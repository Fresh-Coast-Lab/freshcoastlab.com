The differentiator isn't the model. It's the trust.

Thirty seconds on stage at Pax8 Beyond, expanded.

Pax8 Beyond gave me a 30-second quick-fire slot. It's hard to land a message in 30 seconds, so I kept it to one: in the age of AI, the differentiator isn't the model. It's the trust.

Here's the longer version.

Tools change every week

There's a new model, a new agent framework or a new copilot almost every week. If your value is "we have the newest tool," your value expires every week too. Your customers can buy the same tools you can.

People buy from people

What they can't buy is someone who knows their business, tells them the truth, and picks up the phone when something breaks at 2 AM. Relationships are the one thing AI can't automate, at least not yet.

I saw the same thing at my first Ultimate Partner event in Bellevue a few weeks earlier. The content was excellent, but the real work happened in the hallways and at dinner. That's where problems get solved and partnerships get built.

The AI-first shift won't be won by whoever has the best stack. It'll be won by whoever has the strongest relationships. Tech may be the headline. People are why I keep showing up.
