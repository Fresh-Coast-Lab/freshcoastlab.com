A different muscle: when the room is the customers.

Most of my stage time is with partners. This time it was their clients.

As a channel guy, about 95% of my stage time is in front of MSPs. We talk strategy, stack and growth. We speak the same language.

Then TeamLogic IT in Fort Lauderdale invited me to an executive briefing, and the room wasn't MSPs. It was their small and midsize business customers. Business owners. Office managers. People who run companies, not networks.

That is a different muscle entirely.

What I noticed

There is a real gap between what we know AI can do and what small businesses are actually doing with it. Some are already all in without a plan. Some are waiting for permission that nobody is going to give them. Almost nobody had a clear picture of where their data goes when an employee pastes it into a chatbot.

They didn't want a product pitch. They wanted someone to explain it plainly: here's what's useful, here's what's risky, here's where to start.

Why it matters for MSPs

An MSP that puts its vendor partners in front of its customers is doing something smart. It's saying: we're not just your IT provider, we're how you'll figure this out. That's the trusted advisor role everyone talks about, done for real.

If you're an MSP, invite your customers to the conversation. They're more ready than you think, and more confused than they'll admit.
