The archive

Ninety years, one library.

Decades of family photos, phone videos and film reels, scattered across three accounts and a box of reels. Here is what the agents did with them, step by step. Scroll, and every dot is a photo or a clip.

01 · COMBINEGoogle Takeout · exiftool · rclone · Google Photos API

Three accounts, one library.

Three Google accounts of family photos, merged into a single library filed by year and month, with the real capture dates and places recovered from Google's own records.

0photos in one library0GB, organized
02 · DEDUPLICATEPython · best-copy rules · Live Photo filtering

Keep the best copy. Lose the rest.

Every duplicate compared, and only the higher-quality copy kept. Live Photo motion fragments separated out so they never clutter the library.

0Live Photo fragments filtered out
03 · SORTPython · exiftool · APFS clones

Photos here. Home movies there.

Home video pulled out of the photo stream and given its own pipeline, because a forty-second clip of a birthday deserves better than a thumbnail in a grid.

04 · STITCHffmpeg · VideoToolbox · chapter markers

One day, one episode.

Short phone clips from the same day stitched into a single episode with a chapter for every clip. Sideways videos turned upright. Pocket videos and accidental recordings removed.

05 · RESTORECloudflare Workers · R2 · KV

Film reels from the 1930s, finally dated.

Digitized family film reels pulled apart and recombined by trip and occasion, then dated from what is on screen. Relatives identified frame by frame. My mother names the faces on a private page, and every answer flows back into the library.

0reels0films0stills for naming
06 · RECOGNIZEArcFace · on-device matching

A face engine that runs at home.

A face recognition engine on local hardware: every face in every home video embedded and matched against people the family has already named. The matching never leaves the house.

0faces embedded0videos scanned
07 · PRESENTPython · Pillow · ffmpeg

Every video gets a title card.

Each episode opens with a title card in the same style as the family videos I edited back in 2006, with a real title, a real date, and a plain-English description of who and what is in it.

08 · APPROVElaunchd · a watched Done folder

Nothing publishes without my yes.

Finished episodes land in a staging folder. I watch, I approve, and approval is the only thing that publishes: the moment a video lands in Done, it is filed, given artwork and added to the library automatically.

09 · LIBRARYPlex · OpenCV poster picker

A library that keeps learning.

Home movies in Plex, filed by decade through the 1990s and by year after that, with posters picked by a frame scorer that looks for faces. As new names come in, the titles and descriptions keep getting better.

0home videos0collections
